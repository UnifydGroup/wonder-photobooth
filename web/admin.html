Set up the iPad

  1. On the iPad, open in Safari.
  2. Tap Share › Add to Home Screen, then open it from the home screen.
  3. Enter the staff passcode and tap Start booth. Allow camera access.
  4. Turn on Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility) and triple-click to lock the iPad to the booth.
  5. Staff menu on the booth: tap the top-left corner 5 times.

Brand profiles

A profile stores a client's brand kit: event and app names, hashtags, logos, colours, wording and frames. Load one to prefill the settings below, check it, then Save changes. Saving or updating a profile includes any unsaved edits.

Event

The client or occasion this booth is running for. Shown on frames, the booth and the guest page.

App brand

Your own brand (the agency or booth brand). Colours style the booth and guest page.
